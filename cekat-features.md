## AI Chatbot That Chats Like a Human, 24/7

*An AI Chatbot That Understands What Customers Mean, Not Just Their Keywords* — <https://cekat.ai/en/features/ai-chatbot>

The AI chatbot in Cekat.AI is an AI agent that answers customers like a person: it understands the intent and context of a conversation across languages, rather than matching keywords that break the moment a customer phrases something differently. It learns from your business's Knowledge Source, handles sales flows, reads images, processes voice messages, and checks shipping costs in real time, then hands the chat to your team when the conditions you set are met.

**Built to Turn Conversations Into Revenue**

- **Understands Intent, Not Just Keywords** — Keyword chatbots get stuck the moment a customer writes differently. Cekat.AI's AI agent reads intent and context across languages, so “is the black one still available?” gets a real product answer, not a menu of buttons to tap.
- **Handles Complex Tasks, Not Just FAQs** — Sales flows, real-time shipping quotes, reading photos customers send, even processing voice messages. The AI takes the parts of a conversation that usually eat up an admin's time, then hands the rest to your team.
- **Learns from Your Business's Knowledge Source** — Fill the Knowledge Source with text, website pages, PDF files, Q&A pairs, a product catalog, or an API connection. The AI answers from those sources, so replies follow your own policies and prices instead of guesswork.
- **One AI for Many Chats at Once** — A person can only focus on one chat at a time. The AI agent replies to many conversations at once, each with its own context, across WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok DM, and live chat on your website or app.
- **Knows When to Hand Over to a Person** — Set an Agent Transfer Condition, or trigger a handover with labels. When the condition is met, the chat moves to your team with its full history, and supervisors can watch conversations the AI is handling in real time.

**Key Capabilities**

- **Flow for Menus and Buttons, Before the AI Takes Over** — Flow is a decision tree for menus and buttons, such as choosing a branch or a service, before the conversation goes to the AI or a human agent. While you test, a flow can be set to “in dev”, so simulations are safe and do not use AI credits before it officially goes live.
- **Automation for More Complex Flows** — For complex workflows and integrations with internal systems, Cekat.AI has Automation, an n8n-based builder. Custom API connections, Google Sheets, and CRM syncs are set up as custom work with our team.
- **Every Chat Reaches the Right Person** — Round Robin and Least Assign First come built in. Chats can also go to specific agents, AI agents, or teams, including a preferred agent, with the choice to wait or fall back to standard distribution when that person is unavailable. A cap on chats per agent keeps queues manageable.
- **Quiet Chats Closed Cleanly** — AI Auto Resolve and Human Agent Auto Resolve close sessions based on the customer's or agent's last message, and can send a follow-up message first before the session closes automatically.
- **Supervisors See Everything, in Real Time** — Supervisors can watch every conversation the AI is handling in real time under the Unassigned tab, plus every Assigned and Resolved chat in their team, to keep service quality in check.
