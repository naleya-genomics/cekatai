TikTok Ads Integration: Into DMs or WhatsApp

From the FYP Straight Into a Closing Chat

The TikTok Ads integration in Cekat.AI connects TikTok ads to conversations: the TikTok Inbox route takes clickers into TikTok DMs, the Click-to-WhatsApp route takes them to WhatsApp Business, and both land in one inbox with campaign attribution and conversion signals sent back. 24/7 ad leads answered instantly

Built to Turn Conversations Into Revenue

  • Two Routes, One InboxAds into TikTok DMs suit quick questions and bookings; ads into WhatsApp suit sales with a longer follow-up. Whichever way they point, the chats land in the same inbox.
  • Viewers Become Conversations Without Switching AppsThe TikTok Inbox route answers people inside the app they already have open, with opening message templates included. Buying intent does not die in an app switch.
  • The WhatsApp Route for Deals That Need NurturingClick-to-WhatsApp moves serious buyers onto your business number, where AI qualifies, the team takes over, and the CRM records everything through to closing.
  • The Origin Campaign Sticks to Every LeadEvery chat carries its TikTok campaign's name into the CRM. You see what your viral content actually produces: viewers, or buyers.
  • Conversions Sent Back to TikTokDeals closed in chat flow back through the pixel and Events API, so TikTok optimizes toward people who actually buy, not people who merely stop scrolling.

FAQ

What is the TikTok Ads integration in Cekat.AI?

The connection from TikTok ads to conversations via two routes: TikTok Inbox Ads opening TikTok DMs, and Click-to-WhatsApp opening WhatsApp Business chats. Both land in one inbox with campaign attribution.

When should we use the TikTok DM route?

For light lead generation, product consultation, and bookings, when you want to answer people without moving them out of the app they already have open.

When is the WhatsApp route the better fit?

For sales needing intensive follow-up. WhatsApp brings the conversation onto your business number with the CRM, automated follow-ups, and one unified history.

Can both routes run at the same time?

Yes. The route is chosen per campaign, and all the chats still land in the same inbox, so the team never switches tools.

How does attribution work?

Chats arriving from ads carry their origin campaign into the CRM, so you read leads and closings per TikTok campaign without manual recaps.

Are conversions sent back to TikTok?

Yes, through the TikTok Pixel and Events API. Sales that happen in chat become optimization signals, so the next ads find people who resemble your buyers.

Does the AI answer TikTok leads too?

Yes. Chats from both routes are treated the same: answered in the first second, qualified, and handed to the team when ready to close.

Do competitors have TikTok integration too?

Mostly not yet. On our comparison page, TikTok DM and TikTok Ads sit among the rows other platforms most rarely tick.
