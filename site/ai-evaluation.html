AI Feedback & Evaluation: Correct Your AI Agent

Correct It Once, and the AI Stays Corrected

AI Feedback & Evaluation in Cekat.AI is the quality loop for the AI agent: flagging off-target responses from live conversations, testing answers in the AI Playground before they are used, and managing the evaluation history from one page. 24/7 answer quality under watch

Built to Turn Conversations Into Revenue

  • Flag a Bad Answer Without Leaving the ChatFrom a live conversation, the team gives an off-target response a thumbs down. No internal ticket, no complaint spreadsheet: the correction happens where the problem was spotted.
  • Test in the Playground, Not on CustomersThe AI Playground lets you throw questions at the AI and inspect its answers before they reach a real conversation. Knowledge and settings changes get tried first; customers are not the test bench.
  • Every Evaluation Kept, Editable, RemovableThe Evaluation page holds the whole feedback history: what was corrected, what is stale and can go. Your AI's improvement is documented rather than scattered across team chats.
  • An AI That Improves From Real CasesCorrections come from genuine conversations with your customers, not invented scenarios. An answer fixed today prevents the same mistake across the next thousand chats.
  • Your CS Team Becomes the Coach, Not the PatchInstead of taking over the same chat again and again, the team corrects the source once. Time that went into patching AI answers moves to the cases that truly need a human.

FAQ

What is AI Feedback & Evaluation in Cekat.AI?

The AI quality loop: flag off-target responses from live chats with a thumbs down, test answers in the AI Playground, and manage the whole evaluation history from one page.

How do I give feedback on an AI reply?

From a live conversation, hover over the Resolve section and click the thumbs-down icon on the response you want corrected. The evaluation is recorded without leaving the chat.

What is the AI Playground?

A place to test the AI before its answers reach customers. Ask it questions, inspect the responses, and judge the results in an environment separate from real conversations.

Can old evaluations be edited or removed?

Yes. The Evaluation page lists the feedback history for review, editing when a correction needs refining, and removal once it is no longer relevant.

Who should be giving the feedback?

Whoever knows the right answer best, usually CS or a supervisor. Because it is one click from the chat, correcting becomes a daily habit rather than a special project.

How is this different from retraining the knowledge base?

The knowledge base is the source of what the AI knows; evaluation corrects how it answers. They complement each other: feedback often reveals which part of the knowledge needs sharpening.

Does feedback change the AI's answers immediately?

Evaluations are recorded and become managed corrections. You test the change in the Playground first, so no correction ships unreviewed.

Does this feature need technical skill?

No. Feedback is one click, the Playground is an ordinary conversation, and the evaluation page is a searchable list. All of it is built for non-technical teams.
