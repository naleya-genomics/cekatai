Shipping Cost Automation, Instant Ongkir in Chat

Customers Get the Shipping Cost the Moment They Ask

Shipping Cost Automation is a Cekat.AI feature where an AI agent checks shipping costs automatically inside a chat conversation through API integration, giving customers an accurate ongkir in seconds instead of waiting for an admin to look it up.

Built to Turn Conversations Into Revenue

  • Accurate Ongkir Before the Buyer Cools OffThe gap between "how much is shipping?" and an answer is where orders die. Cekat.AI's agent checks the shipping cost the instant a customer asks and quotes it inside the chat, so the buyer sees a real total while they're still ready to pay, not ten minutes later when they've already moved on.
  • No Admin Flipping Between Courier TabsManual shipping lookups mean an admin copying an address, opening a courier site, and typing the rate back, for every single order. Cekat.AI does it through API integration automatically, so your team stops being a human calculator and the customer never waits for a rate that should take one second.
  • Fewer Abandoned CheckoutsEvery unanswered shipping question is an abandoned order in the making. When the ongkir shows up instantly and accurately in the same conversation, buyers keep going instead of drifting off to a seller who replied faster. Instant answers around the clock are how businesses on Cekat.AI lift conversion.
  • Shipping Rolls Straight Into the OrderOnce the ongkir is checked, the AI adds it to the order total and keeps going, collecting details and sending a payment QR without a break. The shipping cost isn't a dead-end question; it's one automatic step in a chat that ends with a completed, paid order.

Key Capabilities

  • Rates Pulled Through API IntegrationCekat.AI connects to shipping through API integration, so the AI checks the real cost during the conversation instead of guessing or asking the customer to wait. The rate is accurate and it arrives in seconds.
  • The Quote Never Leaves the ConversationThe customer asks about shipping and gets the answer right there, no redirect to a website, no "let me check and get back to you." The whole exchange stays inside the chat on whatever channel they used.
  • Shipping Added to the Total AutomaticallyThe checked ongkir folds straight into the order the AI is building, so the customer sees one clear total, items plus shipping, and can pay immediately with the QR sent in the same chat.
  • Quotes at Any HourBuyers ask about shipping at midnight too. The AI quotes the cost around the clock, so an order placed after your team logs off still moves forward instead of stalling until morning.

FAQ

What is shipping cost automation?

Shipping cost automation is when an AI checks shipping rates for a customer automatically instead of an admin looking them up by hand. In Cekat.AI, the AI agent checks the ongkir through API integration during the chat and quotes an accurate cost in seconds, then folds it into the order.

How does Cekat.AI check the shipping cost?

Through API integration. When a customer asks about shipping, the AI reads the destination from the conversation, checks the rate via the connected shipping API, and quotes the accurate cost right in the chat, no admin, no manual lookup, no delay while the buyer waits.

Is the shipping cost accurate?

Yes. Because the rate comes through API integration rather than a manual estimate, the customer sees the real shipping cost. That accuracy matters, a wrong quote either eats your margin or surprises the buyer at payment and loses the sale entirely.

How fast do customers get the ongkir?

In seconds. The AI checks and quotes the shipping cost the moment the customer asks, inside the same conversation. There's no queue behind other chats and no waiting for an admin to be free, which is exactly what keeps a ready buyer from abandoning the order.

Does automatic shipping check reduce abandoned orders?

It helps. Many orders are lost in the silence after "how much is shipping?" When the ongkir appears instantly and accurately in the same chat, buyers keep going instead of leaving for a faster seller, and the AI moves straight on to collecting payment.

What channels does the shipping check work on?

All of them. WhatsApp via the official Business API, Instagram DM, Facebook Messenger, website live chat, and API channels, every conversation lands in one unified inbox and the AI can check and quote shipping on whichever channel the customer used.

Does the shipping cost get added to the order automatically?

Yes. After checking the ongkir, the AI adds it to the order total and continues the flow, collecting customer details and sending a payment QR in the same chat. The shipping quote is one automatic step toward a completed, paid order.

Does it work outside business hours?

Yes. The AI quotes shipping 24/7. With AI working hours, your team handles chats during office hours and the AI takes over after, so a customer asking about ongkir at night gets an instant answer instead of waiting until your team is back online.

Do I need to code the shipping integration myself?

No coding is required on your side. Cekat.AI handles shipping through API integration, and you set up the AI by pasting your business info into the knowledge base and shaping conversations with a visual drag-and-drop flow designer, building an agent takes around five minutes.
