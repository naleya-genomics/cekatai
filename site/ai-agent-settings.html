Advanced AI Agent Settings: Memory, Tone, Guardrails

An AI You Can Tune, Not a Black Box

Advanced AI settings in Cekat.AI are the behavior controls of the AI agent: limits on history, context, and files read, answer creativity level, reply delays and message caps, a quality watcher, timezone, and session-only memory. ~5 min to set up a bot, no code 24/7 AI behavior consistent with your rules

Built to Turn Conversations Into Revenue

  • Decide How Far Back the AI RemembersMessage history limits, knowledge-source context limits, and file reading limits are yours to set. Long conversations stay relevant without the AI dragging in a topic from three days ago.
  • A Reply Style That Follows Your BusinessThree temperature presets: Consistent for the same answer every time, Balanced for the everyday, Creative for looser conversation. A clinic and a fashion brand should not sound alike.
  • A Watcher Guarding Answer QualityThe watcher monitors your AI's responses and action execution. When something drifts from expectations, you hear it from the system, not from a customer complaint a week later.
  • A Reply Rhythm That Feels HumanMessage wait adds a pause before the AI answers, so customers finish their burst of messages first. A per-conversation response cap keeps the AI from monopolizing chats that need a human.
  • Session Memory and the Right TimezoneSession-only memory restricts recall to the running conversation, useful for topics that must not carry over. Timezone keeps promised hours and scheduling on your business clock.

FAQ

What can the advanced AI settings control?

Message history limits, knowledge-source context limits, file reading limits, answer temperature, reply delay, response caps, the watcher, timezone, and session-only memory.

What is AI temperature?

The variation level of answers. Consistent keeps replies nearly identical every time, right for policy and pricing; Balanced suits the everyday; Creative allows improvisation for casual conversation.

What does the watcher do?

It monitors response quality and the AI's action execution: a supervising layer so drift gets caught by the system rather than by your customers.

When is session-only memory useful?

When the AI should not carry information between conversations, such as sensitive data or closed cases. Recall is limited to the running session.

What is message wait for?

A pause before the AI replies. Customers often type several messages in a burst; the pause lets the AI answer the whole thought once instead of interrupting each message.

Do these settings need a developer?

No. Everything is adjusted from the AI agent dashboard through clearly labeled options, and can be changed again anytime without a redeploy.

Do settings apply per agent or per account?

Per agent. A sales AI and a support AI can carry different memory, style, and limits while running on the same account.

How does this relate to AI Working Hours?

AI Working Hours decides when the AI is active; advanced settings decide how it behaves while active. Used together they give full control.
