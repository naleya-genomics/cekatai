WhatsApp Blast via Official API, Reach Thousands in One Send

Reach Thousands of Customers in One WhatsApp Blast

WhatsApp Blast is a Cekat.AI feature that sends broadcast messages to thousands of customers at once through the official WhatsApp Business API, with audience segmentation, automated reminders, and conversion tracking built into one platform.

Built to Turn Conversations Into Revenue

  • Segment Your Audience Before You Hit SendBlasting the same message to everyone is how businesses get ignored. Cekat.AI segments your audience by activity, purchase history, or events, so a repeat buyer gets a loyalty offer while a ghosting lead gets a nudge back to checkout.
  • Send Promos That Feel Personal, Not SpammyEvery blast pulls the customer's name and context from your CRM, so "Hi Steven, your promo ends December 10" lands instead of a generic announcement. Campaigns that feel relevant get opened, clicked, and answered.
  • Follow Up Automatically When Customers Go QuietThe money is in the follow-up, and Cekat.AI never forgets one. Automatic reminders go out before key moments, unpaid orders, expiring promos, replenishment time, based on each customer's actual behavior.
  • Measure Replies, Conversions, and Revenue, Not Just SendsA blast that doesn't sell is just noise. Track open rates, replies, and the revenue each campaign generates in one analytics dashboard, then double down on the segments and messages that actually convert.

Key Capabilities

  • Sent Through Meta's Official InfrastructureCekat.AI is an official Meta Business Partner. Blasts go out through the WhatsApp Business API with approved message templates, the compliant way to message customers at scale, without the risks that come with unofficial blast tools.
  • Audiences Built From Real BehaviorSegment by activity, purchase history, or event, first-time buyers, repeat customers, leads who clicked but never paid. Every campaign goes to the people it was actually written for.
  • Event-Based Reminders on AutopilotPayment still pending? Promo ending? Restock time? Cekat.AI sends the right reminder at the right moment automatically, so no revenue slips away because your team was busy.

FAQ

What is a WhatsApp blast?

A WhatsApp blast is a broadcast message sent to many customers at once on WhatsApp. With Cekat.AI, blasts are sent through the official WhatsApp Business API using approved templates, segmented by customer behavior, and tracked through to replies and conversions.

Is WhatsApp blast with Cekat.AI safe for my number?

Yes. Cekat.AI is an official Meta Business Partner and sends every campaign through the official WhatsApp Business API with Meta-approved message templates, the compliant method for bulk messaging, unlike unofficial tools that put your number at risk.

How is this different from WhatsApp Business broadcast lists?

The free WhatsApp Business app limits broadcasts to small lists and only reaches people who saved your number. The official API through Cekat.AI reaches your full customer list, personalizes each message, and adds segmentation, automation, and analytics.

Can I choose exactly who receives each campaign?

Yes. Audiences are segmented by activity, purchase history, or events, for example, customers who bought last month, leads who never completed payment, or everyone who joined a recent promo. Each campaign targets only the segment you pick.

Can the messages be personalized?

Every message can pull customer data like names and order details from the CRM, so each recipient gets a message that reads like it was written for them, which is exactly why personalized campaigns get more opens and replies.

What happens when customers reply to a blast?

Replies land in the Cekat.AI inbox, where your AI agent answers instantly, 24/7, in natural language. It can answer questions, check shipping costs, and close the order right in the chat, escalating to your team only when needed.

Can I see whether a campaign actually made money?

Yes. The analytics dashboard tracks each campaign from send to reply to conversion, so you see revenue per campaign, not just delivery counts. Winning segments and messages become obvious, and budget follows what works.

Do I need technical skills to run a blast?

No. Building a campaign is point-and-click: pick a segment, write the message, send or automate it. No coding is needed anywhere in Cekat.AI, most teams run their first campaign the same day they start.

Does WhatsApp blast work together with the other Cekat.AI products?

Yes, that's the point. Blasts use CRM data for segmentation, the AI agent handles replies, order automation closes sales in chat, and results feed back into your customer profiles. One platform covers the whole loop.
