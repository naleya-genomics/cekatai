FAQ

What is the UTM & wa.me Generator in Cekat.AI?

A tool for building trackable links without hand-assembling parameters: UTM links for any page and wa.me links that open a chat with your business WhatsApp, both with a source readable through to the CRM.

How is it different from building UTMs by hand?

The parameters are the same; the difference is consistency. The form keeps naming uniform, so reports stop splitting over spelling or capitalization differences between teammates.

What are tracked wa.me links for?

So chats arriving via a bio, a poster, or creator content reveal their door. Without them, every WhatsApp chat looks identical and the channel doing the work never gets credit.

Can it measure UGC and creator content?

Yes, that is one of its main uses. Give each creator their own link and you read chats and closings per creator, not just their content's impressions.

Are custom parameters supported?

Yes. Beyond the standard source, medium, and campaign, you can add your own parameters to match how you report.

Where do the clicks show up?

Clicks and visits are read through Cekat Tracker on the performance dashboards, and chats born from the link carry their source into the inbox and CRM.

Does the source persist to the sale?

Yes. Because the source attaches to the contact from the first chat, a closing that happens later still traces back to the originating link.

Does it need technical skill?

No. Filling the form and copying the link is as easy as shortening a URL, and wa.me links need nothing installed on a website at all.
