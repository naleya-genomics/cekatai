FAQ

What is the WhatsApp Business API?

The WhatsApp Business API is Meta's official platform for medium and large-scale business messaging on WhatsApp. Unlike the free app, it has no single-phone limit, it connects your number to software like Cekat.AI, where a whole team plus an AI agent can send, receive, and automate messages on one number.

What's the difference between the WhatsApp Business app and the API?

The free WhatsApp Business app is built for one person on one phone, with manual replies and small broadcast lists. The API removes those limits: unlimited team members on one number, Meta-approved templates for outbound campaigns, automation triggers, and AI agents, all managed through a platform like Cekat.AI rather than a phone.

How do I get access to the WhatsApp Business API?

Access goes through Meta's official channels, typically via a Business Partner. Cekat.AI is an official Meta Business Partner, so you sign up, and the Cekat.AI team guides business verification, number registration, and template approval until your API number is live and answering customers.

Can my whole team use one WhatsApp number at the same time?

Yes, that's one of the main reasons businesses move to the API. In Cekat.AI, every teammate logs into the same shared inbox with role-based access. You can add collaborators to specific conversations, leave private notes, and prioritize cases, all on a single official number.

Is the WhatsApp Business API safe for my number?

Yes, when accessed officially. Cekat.AI is an official Meta Business Partner, so messages travel through Meta's own infrastructure with approved templates, the compliant route for business messaging. That's the opposite of unofficial blast tools, which operate outside Meta's rules and put numbers at risk.

Do I need a developer to use the WhatsApp Business API?

No. The API itself is technical, but Cekat.AI wraps it in a no-code platform: a visual drag-and-drop chat-flow designer, a knowledge base you fill by pasting SOPs, and an AI agent you can build in about 5 minutes. It's as easy as briefing a CS admin.

What can the AI agent do on my WhatsApp number?

It answers customers 24/7 in natural language, understanding intent and context rather than keywords. It can respond in multiple languages, check shipping costs, send payment QR codes, create orders from the conversation, and escalate to your team with full context when a human is needed.

Can I send broadcast campaigns through the API?

Yes. Meta-approved templates let you message customers first at scale, promos, payment reminders, replenishment nudges. In Cekat.AI, campaigns are segmented by activity, purchase history, or events, and every reply comes back to the same inbox where AI answers it.

What happens to conversations outside working hours?

Cekat.AI's AI working hours mirror your team's schedule: while your team is online, they lead; after hours, the AI takes over automatically. When your team returns, handoff carries the full conversation context, so customers never repeat themselves and no overnight lead goes cold.

Is customer data on the API handled securely?

Cekat.AI complies with UU PDP, Indonesia's personal data protection law, and runs WhatsApp messaging through Meta's official Business API infrastructure. Conversations, customer profiles, and campaign data stay inside one governed platform with role-based team access instead of scattered across personal phones.
