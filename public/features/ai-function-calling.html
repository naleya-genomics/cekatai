AI Function Calling: AI That Acts in Your Systems

AI That Finishes the Transaction, Not Just the Conversation

Function calling in Cekat.AI is the AI agent's ability to invoke real functions while a conversation is happening: creating orders from chat content, issuing payment via payment links or manual transfer, and executing actions in external systems over API. 24/7 AI executing orders and payments ~5 min to set up a bot, no code

Built to Turn Conversations Into Revenue

  • Chat Becomes an Order Without Touching a Dashboard

    The moment a customer commits, the AI collects the name, address, and order details from the conversation and creates the order. No copy-paste into another system, no order lost to a busy admin.

  • Shipping and Couriers Handled Inside the Conversation

    Make the shipping address mandatory or optional, choose the sender address and courier. The AI calculates and confirms all of it before the order is created, without leaving the chat.

  • The Bill Goes Out the Moment They Say Yes

    Choose auto-generated payment links or route to the bank accounts and e-wallets you register. The riskiest gap in any sale, between agreeing and paying, shrinks to a single message.

  • Call Your Own APIs

    Function calling does not stop at orders. Connect your stock, booking, or internal system APIs and the AI can check and act on them mid-conversation, like an employee with system access.

  • Every Channel, One Execution Path

    Orders from WhatsApp, Instagram, or website live chat run through the same flow. Configure the function once and every channel you sell on gets it.

FAQ

What is function calling in Cekat.AI?

The AI agent's ability to invoke real functions mid-chat: creating orders from the conversation, issuing payment, and executing actions over API. The AI acts rather than only answering.

How is it different from Order Automation?

Order Automation is the order flow itself; function calling is the machinery that lets the AI execute that flow and other actions beyond orders, including custom functions over API.

How does the AI know what data to collect?

From the function's configuration. For orders, it asks for customer details, the address when you make it mandatory, and confirmation, then calls the function once everything is complete.

Which payment methods are supported?

Two modes: a payment link generated automatically per order, or manual payment routed to the bank accounts and e-wallets you register yourself.

Can the AI call APIs beyond the built-in features?

Yes. Through the Open API you register your own functions, stock checks, schedule bookings, or internal system queries, and the AI calls them when the conversation needs to.

What happens when a function call fails?

The failure is visible in the conversation rather than swallowed silently, so the team can step in. The case can escalate to a human agent with full context.

Do we need a developer to turn it on?

Not for orders and payments; enable it from the Integrations tab and set the options from the dashboard. A developer only comes in when you connect your own custom API.

Which channels support function calling?

Every chat channel connected to Cekat.AI, including WhatsApp, Instagram, and website live chat. Configure a function once and it applies across channels.
