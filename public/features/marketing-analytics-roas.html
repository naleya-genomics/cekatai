Marketing Analytics & ROAS Dashboard

Know Exactly Which Campaigns Make You Money

Marketing analytics in Cekat.AI is a unified dashboard that tracks ad spend, ROAS, cost, revenue, and customer progression across your campaigns, connecting marketing activity to real business outcomes so teams can see what works and optimize with confidence.

Built to Turn Conversations Into Revenue

  • One Dashboard, Not Ten Browser Tabs

    Ad spend in one place, revenue in another, chats somewhere else, that scatter is how insights get lost. Cekat.AI puts ad spend, ROAS, cost, and revenue in a single dashboard, so you see the full picture of marketing performance without stitching reports together.

  • Connect Spend to Actual Revenue

    Clicks and impressions don't pay the bills. The dashboard ties campaign spend to the revenue it produced, so you finally see ROAS that reflects real sales, not vanity metrics, and know which campaigns earn their budget.

  • Watch Customers Progress From Click to Buyer

    See how customers move through stages, visitor, lead, buyer, after they engage your marketing. Understanding that progression shows you where people drop off and where to focus, instead of judging campaigns on clicks alone.

  • Allocate Budget With Confidence

    When you can see what each campaign actually earns, budget decisions stop being guesses. Double down on the audiences and campaigns that drive revenue, cut the ones that only look busy, and lower your acquisition cost over time.

FAQ

What does the marketing analytics dashboard show?

It shows ad spend, ROAS, cost, revenue, and customer progression together in one place. Instead of checking separate tools, you see how your campaigns perform and how customers move from click to purchase in a single dashboard.

What is ROAS and how does Cekat.AI track it?

ROAS (return on ad spend) measures revenue earned per unit of ad spend. Cekat.AI ties campaign spend to the actual revenue it generated, so your ROAS reflects real sales rather than clicks or impressions.

How is this different from the analytics inside each ad platform?

Ad platforms show their own clicks and costs in isolation. Cekat.AI unifies spend and real revenue across campaigns and connects them to customer progression, giving you outcome-based insight the individual platforms can't show alone.

Can I see which campaigns actually drive sales?

Yes. Because the dashboard connects marketing activity to real business outcomes, you can clearly see which campaigns generate leads, conversions, and revenue, and which ones only produce clicks.

Does it track customer behavior after the click?

Yes. You can see how customers progress through stages like visitor, lead, and buyer after engaging your marketing, so you understand where they drop off and where to focus.

How does this help me lower acquisition cost?

By showing what each campaign actually earns, the dashboard helps you shift budget to the audiences and campaigns that drive revenue and cut the ones that don't, reducing wasted spend and lowering CAC over time.

Does it work with Conversion API tracking?

Yes. Real conversions sent to Meta and TikTok via Conversion API feed the same analytics, so the dashboard reflects accurate, complete performance data including sales that close in chat.

Do I need an analyst to use it?

No. The dashboard presents ad spend, ROAS, revenue, and progression in a clear view any marketer or business owner can read, so you can make decisions without a dedicated data analyst.
