Agentic AI That Acts, Not Just Replies

Agentic AI that acts, not just replies.

One AI agent, plenty of work done on its own

  • From “is it in stock?” to invoice sent

    The agent answers product questions, creates the order, works out shipping to the buyer's address, and sends the invoice. No admin has to step in.

  • Payment in, order status updated

    Once the buyer pays by QR in the chat, the payment is recorded, the order status changes, and the agent lets the buyer know. No more matching transfers one by one.

  • Complaints logged, the right team in the loop

    The agent recognizes the complaint, labels it, and passes it to the team in charge with the whole conversation attached. Your team can track it as a ticket until it's resolved.

  • No lead slips through

    The agent follows up with prospects who haven't replied, then passes the ones ready to buy to your sales team.

What makes this agent actually work

  • Takes real action in your systems

    The agent doesn't stop at an answer. It calls the functions you've connected to get the job done.

  • Learns from your own sources

    Train the agent on text, websites, files, Q&A, your product catalog, or an API connection. Its answers follow your business, not generic replies.

  • Knows when to stop

    Decide when a chat goes to a team or a specific department. Until that condition is met, the agent works on its own.

    Decide how the agent talks and how far it can go.

  • Connect channels

FAQ

What is agentic AI?

Agentic AI is an AI agent that doesn't stop at an answer. It understands the context of a conversation, then takes real action in the connected systems, such as creating an order, quoting shipping, or updating your CRM, without being told what to do at every step.

How is it different from a regular chatbot?

A chatbot runs on rules and keywords, and stops at the answer. Conversational AI understands everyday language, but the follow-up is still manual. Agentic AI understands what the customer wants, then finishes the process itself.

Can it connect to the systems I already use?

Yes. The AI agent answers on WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, and live chat, while email, Telegram, social media comments, and Google Play reviews arrive in the same inbox. Payments use the built-in Xendit integration, and other systems such as CRMs or Google Sheets connect through Automation or webhooks as a custom setup.

How does the agent know when to hand over to a person?

You set the conditions when you configure the agent, for example when a customer complains, when approval is needed, or when a question is beyond the agent's knowledge. The chat then goes to the right team or department, with the full context.

Can one agent handle many chats at once?

Yes. The agent serves many conversations at the same time with no queue, and each conversation keeps its own context. A spike in chats doesn't leave customers waiting.

What is AI Orchestration?

AI Orchestration moves a chat between AI agents automatically, based on conditions you write. You arrange a main agent and specialist agents on a canvas, add a condition to each card, and the chat moves to whichever agent's condition matches: from the main agent to a specialist, between specialists, or back to the main agent.

How is customer data protected?

Cekat.AI is certified to ISO/IEC 27001:2022 for information security and ISO 9001:2015 for quality management, and complies with Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law No. 27 of 2022.

Do I need technical skills?

Not for the basic setup. Training the agent, shaping its behavior, and connecting channels all happen in the dashboard without code. Integrations with external systems are best done with your technical team, and our team is happy to help set them up.
